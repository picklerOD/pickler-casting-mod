## Install

1. Download `Pickler_Casting_Mod.luau`.
2. Put it in your Orion Drift `Cameras/Behaviors` folder. To update, replace the old file.
3. Select Pickler in the game and open **START** in its menu. Press **Tab** to hide or show the menu.

## What's inside

### Cameras

| Style | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Plutus** | Orbits the action, and can jump into first person when someone's dribbling |
| **Zepy** | TV-style broadcast camera that's aware of the crowd and goals |
| **Yuki** | Slides along the sideline with the ball and swaps sides as play crosses over |
| **Snowman** | Sits behind the ball carrier, or chases the ball when nobody has it |

You can also follow one player in first or third person, or fly a free camera.

### Goal explosions

39 explosions, plus Shuffle and Shuffle Favorites:

- **Big hits:** Dragon Duel, Supernova, Meteor Strike, Phoenix Rising, Jackpot, Light Show and more
- **Specials:** Death Note, GG EZ, Busted, Paparazzi, UFO Abduction and more
- **Holidays:** sets for Halloween, Christmas, New Year's and Valentine's

### Also included

- Kickoff countdown with a GO burst
- Ball trails and in-game shaders
- Voice chat and player controls
- Saved presets
- Automatic goal clips with OBS or Medal, using the optional Clip Companion for Windows

### One-click setups

| Setup | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Recommended** | Plutus camera, a beam trail and a random explosion every goal |
| **Clean Broadcast** | Zepy camera with no explosions: just the match |
| **Low-End PC** | Lowest effect quality for a smoother frame rate |
| **Easy on the Eyes** | Softer flashes and no camera shake |
