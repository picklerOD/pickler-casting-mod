Pick how you want to cast

Let the camera run itself with one of four styles, stay locked on one player in first or third person, or fly a free camera yourself.

Plutus

An orbit camera that swings around the action, and can jump into first person when someone's dribbling.

Zepy

A TV-style broadcast camera that also knows about the crowd and the goals around it.

Yuki

A sideline camera that slides along the arena with the ball and swaps sides as play crosses over.

Snowman

Sits behind the ball carrier, or chases the ball when nobody has it.

Choose a favorite, star the ones you like, or let Shuffle surprise you with a different explosion every goal. Press TEST IT to see one without waiting for a goal.
