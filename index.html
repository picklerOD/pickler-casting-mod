Pickler Casting Mod

A script that makes casting Orion Drift look like a real broadcast.

Download

Put the file in your Orion Drift Cameras/Behaviors folder, then select Pickler in the game.

What it is

Pickler is a spectator camera script. It follows the play for you, adds effects to the big moments, and can save your clips, so you can focus on calling the game.

Automatic cameras
Several camera styles that follow the ball and the action on their own.
Player views
First and third person on any player.
39 goal explosions
Big hits, joke specials and holiday sets. Pick one, star your favorites or shuffle them.
Kickoff countdown
A countdown over the ball, then a GO burst when play starts.
Automatic clips
Saves each goal with OBS or Medal using the optional Clip Companion for Windows.
Quick setup
A START page gets you going in a few clicks, and your settings save themselves.
Made by Pickler. Camera work adapted from dennssen's Follow+, Plutus' autocaster, Yuki's sideline cam and Snowman's CastCam (Snoowman & nikita.26). Not affiliated with Another Axiom.
